### The Domus Romana connection (possibly the strongest story)

The San Valentino post says the dinner was held in the "suggestiva sala del museo della Domus Romana, con ingresso e collegamento diretto al nostro ristorante". So the restaurant has a direct link to the Domus Romana, the Roman house museum in Lucca, with brick vaulted rooms and ancient walls. No other restaurant in Lucca can say this, and it makes a natural centrepiece for events and private dinners.

## 4. The place

- **Entrance:** a rusticated stone arch on Via San Giorgio, with a black arched sign: "RISTORANTE" in small gold capitals, "Calici" in gold script and "ALTI" in spaced capitals.
- **Inside:** dark wood panelling, pink and cream striped walls behind the bar, yellow and white striped lampshades, a marble-topped bar with wine and spirits shelves, wooden wine cabinets, a chalkboard, and bentwood chairs with cane backs.
- **Tables:** white or linen beige tablecloths, small glass vases with dried flowers, and wine glasses engraved with the Calici Alti logo.
- **Outside:** tables under umbrellas with plants (one Tripadvisor reviewer calls it a pleasant terrace). It's away from the busiest tourist streets.
- **Air conditioning:** yes ("ambienti climatizzati").
- **Feel:** "un angolo nascosto nel cuore di Lucca", classy but relaxed. Reviewers say "lovely restaurant with a classy interior".

## 9. Brand clues

- **Logo:** a hand-drawn gold brush circle with "Calici" in gold script and "ALTI" in spaced serif capitals, on black. The Instagram avatar is a different, busier version: gold line drawing of Lucca towers and two glasses, with "CALICI ALTI" in capitals. The circle version is the main one: it's on the sign, the engraved glasses, the catering logo and the old site. **[ask for the logo as a vector file (SVG, AI or PDF); the old site only has a 300x220 JPG]**
- **Colours seen:** black, champagne gold, white tablecloths, wine red (glasses, roses, red carpet), warm dark wood, blush pink stripes.
- **Type on the sign:** an elegant script plus classic spaced serif capitals. Pair a refined serif for headlines with a clean sans for text. Keep script for the logo only, never for menus or body text (see first-draft rules).
- **Signature shapes:** the stone arch of the doorway (like Vanni, so handle it differently here) or, better, the brush circle from the logo, the rim of a raised glass.
- **Their Instagram style:** close-up food, hands raising glasses, slow pours, a white t-shirt that reads "the future is female". Photos and videos by @localup.italia (a content agency).
